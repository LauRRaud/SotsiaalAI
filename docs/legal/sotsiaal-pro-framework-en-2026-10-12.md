# Sotsiaal.pro framework for professional use

Version: 2026-10-12

There are two texts here. The first is a short notice to an employee who uses Sotsiaal.pro in the work tasks of their organisation. The second is the framework agreement between the organisation and Sotsiaal.pro, together with its annexes. The employee is not a party to the framework agreement.

# Notice to the professional user

## Why is this notice shown?

Your Organisation gives you the right to use Sotsiaal.pro professionally. This notice explains briefly which content is work-related, which may be private, who sees the data and what the user is responsible for.

This notice is not the Sotsiaal.pro framework agreement on organisational use. You do not become a party to that agreement merely by being a User. Confirming the notice is not consent to the processing of personal data where the processing is based on the law, a work task, a contract or another legal basis.

## What may Sotsiaal.pro be used for professionally?

Sotsiaal.pro can help to find and understand information, organise the work process, draft and improve texts, analyse documents and prepare summaries or other workflows activated by the Organisation.

Sotsiaal.pro is not an official register and does not make decisions on a person's rights, services, benefits, treatment or personnel matters, or other binding decisions. If an entry must reach an official register or records management system, you enter it there yourself in accordance with the Organisation's procedure. Artificial intelligence output may contain errors. Before any official use or use affecting a person's rights, the responsible specialist checks the factual accuracy, relevance, legal basis and confidentiality of the output.

## Which content is the Organisation's Work Data?

The Organisation's Work Data are content that you create or receive for the performance of a work task in a workflow activated by the Organisation. The Organisation determines the purpose of their processing, the legal basis, the permitted users, access and retention. As a rule, Sotsiaal.pro processes these data as the Organisation's processor. Work Data also include entries kept in a work tool, for example a home care diary, a care plan, a Service Log entry or a pre-inquiry that has arrived at the intake desk.

The Organisation's administrative user does not, merely by virtue of their role, gain automatic access to the entire content of your conversations, documents or files. Access must arise from an actual workflow and a technically verified right.

## Which content may remain private?

If the Organisation has activated a private reflection, work wellbeing, co-vision, supervision or mentoring work tool, the private notebook created in it remains, by default, yours alone. The Organisation does not see the original entry unless you knowingly share it yourself or unless a separately activated workflow, the legal basis and the information given to the user clearly provide otherwise.

The Organisation may be shown a separately agreed aggregate that is anonymous or based on a sufficient sample. The aggregate does not give the right to view the original entries or to draw an individual work-related or personnel conclusion about you.

## What must not be entered?

Enter only the information necessary for the work task. If the purpose can be achieved without a name, personal identification code, exact address or other directly identifying information, use a generalised or pseudonymised description.

Do not enter passwords, PIN codes, authentication codes, bank card details, access keys or other secret access credentials into a conversation, document, file or note.

Identifiable data, special categories of data or data that are sensitive for another reason may be used only in a work task permitted by the Organisation and in an activated workflow.

## How does sharing work?

Private content does not automatically become visible to the Organisation or to another party. When sharing, check the recipient and the content to be sent before confirming. A copy that the recipient has opened or transferred into their own proceedings does not automatically disappear when you later delete the source copy.

## Whom to contact?

For questions about a work task, the right of use, the Organisation's Work Data, official retention or a data subject's request, first turn to the Organisation's contact.

For questions about your account, the security of Sotsiaal.pro or a private work tool visible only to you, write to info@sotsiaal.pro.

If you do not know which party the question belongs to, you may turn to either party. The Organisation and Sotsiaal.pro coordinate the reply and do not refer you from one party to the other without good reason.

Report a security flaw, incorrect access or a possible data leak immediately through the channel designated by the Organisation or to info@sotsiaal.pro. Do not add to the report more sensitive content than is necessary to identify the incident.

## Brief summary of the agreement

1. The agreement is intended for the professional use of Sotsiaal.pro by a local government, a service provider, another organisation or an independently operating professional. An employee or other end user is not a party to the agreement merely by being a User.
2. The same agreement is suitable both for a limited Pilot and for Production Use. The mode, users, Modules, data categories, price, metrics and termination terms are selected in the Application Annex.
3. A function that has not been activated or is technically hidden is not part of the service provided to the Organisation, and the Organisation's real data are not processed in it.
4. Sotsiaal.pro helps to find information, structure work, prepare texts and summaries, and organise and document the provision of a service. Sotsiaal.pro does not make decisions concerning a person's rights or services and does not replace the responsibility of a specialist. An entry kept in a work tool, for example a home care diary or a Service Log entry, is the Organisation's operational record, not an official register.
5. A person's private use of Sotsiaal.pro, including their private Journey, does not fall under this agreement. If a person knowingly hands over to the Organisation information they have compiled themselves, the copy received by the Organisation falls within the Organisation's area of responsibility from the moment of handover. The person's source space does not thereby become visible to the Organisation.
6. For the Organisation's Work Data, the Organisation is as a rule the controller and Sotsiaal.pro the processor. For account, billing and security data and for certain private work tools of the user, Sotsiaal.pro may be a separate controller. The specific allocation is in Annex 2.
7. The Organisation's Work Data are not used to train Sotsiaal.pro's or a general-purpose artificial intelligence model. Sotsiaal.pro does not voluntarily include the Organisation's data in an artificial intelligence service provider's model training or data sharing programme.
8. Call recording is a separately selectable Module. It is switched off by default and may not be used before the purpose, legal basis, notification of participants, access, retention period and deletion have been agreed in the Application Annex.
9. The Organisation does not gain automatic access to an employee's private work wellbeing, reflection, co-vision, supervision or mentoring entries. A possible aggregate view and its recipient must be activated separately, and the aggregate does not give the right to view the original entries.
10. This brief summary helps in reading the agreement, but in the event of a conflict the main text of the agreement and the annexes prevail.

# 1. Definitions

1.1. Activation and Application Annex, or Application Annex, means a document drawn up on the basis of Annex 6 and confirmed by the Parties, in which the terms of a specific Pilot or Production Use are selected.

1.2. Data Protection Law means the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the Estonian Personal Data Protection Act and other personal data protection requirements applicable to the specific processing.

1.3. User means an employee, official, contractual worker or volunteer of the Organisation, or another natural person authorised by the Organisation to use Sotsiaal.pro professionally.

1.4. Module means a distinguishable function or workflow of Sotsiaal.pro, for example conversation, document drafting, pre-inquiry intake, the casework desk, the home care work tool, field work, the Service Log, the service profile, work wellbeing, co-vision, supervision, mentoring, a room or a call.

1.5. Organisation means a legal person, public authority, sole proprietor or other natural person that has entered into the agreement and uses Sotsiaal.pro solely in its independent professional or economic activity. Private use by a person acting as a consumer does not fall under the definition of Organisation or under this agreement.

1.6. Organisation's Work Data means personal data and other content that Sotsiaal.pro processes on behalf of the Organisation for the performance of a work task agreed between the Parties. A User's private work tool content is not automatically considered part of the Organisation's Work Data.

1.7. Pilot means use that is limited in time or in terms of users, data, Modules or work process, the purpose of which is to verify suitability, security, impact and the prerequisites for adoption before a decision on Production Use.

1.8. Private work tool content means a work wellbeing, reflection, supervision, mentoring or other personal entry created by a User for themselves, to which the Organisation has no access by default. The exact allocation of roles for private content and its use are described in Annex 2 and, where necessary, in the Application Annex.

1.9. Production Use means the use of Sotsiaal.pro as part of the Organisation's ordinary work arrangements to the extent agreed in the Application Annex.

1.10. Artificial intelligence service provider means a sub-processor of Sotsiaal.pro or another service provider whose technology is used to process text, audio, documents or other inputs and to produce output.

1.11. Handed-over content means a copy of information knowingly sent to or shared with the Organisation by a person or another party. The handover does not give the Organisation access to the rest of the sender's private workspace.

1.12. Journey means a person's private digital workspace for bringing together the thoughts, notes, documents, possible services, questions and next steps related to one life event or concern. The content of a Journey becomes the Organisation's Work Data only to the extent that the person knowingly hands it over to the Organisation.

1.13. Service Log means a professional work tool in which a verified entry created in the course of work or the provision of a service can be used to prepare several agreed outputs, for example a summary, a report, an annex to an invoice or an input to be transmitted to an official system. The Service Log is not in itself an official register or a permanent archive.

1.14. Home care work tool means a Module in which the Organisation organises and documents the provision of home care: the client card, the diary, the care plan, the visit plan, decision and volume data, incidents, and the records of keys, client cash and supplies. The entries of the home care work tool are the Organisation's Work Data. The work tool is not an official register or a permanent archive.

1.15. Casework desk means a User's tool for organising work, used for keeping cases, preparations for meetings and drafts to be entered into an official system. The casework desk is not a client register or an official file, and Sotsiaal.pro does not transmit its content to an official system.

1.16. Intake desk means the Organisation's shared view at which pre-inquiries sent to the Organisation arrive and where the employees designated by the Organisation accept them, reply to them or decline to accept them with an explanation.

# 2. Purpose, scope and parties of the agreement

2.1. The agreement lays down the basic terms of the professional organisational use of Sotsiaal.pro, the activation of the service, the Pilot and Production Use, the processing of personal data, security, termination and the liability of the Parties.

2.2. The parties to the agreement are the Organisation and Sotsiaal.pro. A User is not a party to the agreement merely by being a User. If the Organisation is an independently operating professional, the same natural person may be the Organisation as a party to the agreement and a User when using the service.

Before professional access is activated, the User is presented with a separate short notice on professional use and is asked to confirm that they have received and understood it. The User's confirmation does not mean entering into this agreement or consenting to the processing of personal data where the processing is based on another legal basis. The Organisation may use the usage rules of the agreement as the basis for its internal decision, instructions and the notice to the User.

2.3. The agreement is a data processing agreement within the meaning of Article 28 of the Data Protection Law to the extent that Sotsiaal.pro processes the Organisation's Work Data on behalf of the Organisation.

2.4. The agreement does not govern a person's private use of Sotsiaal.pro, their private Journey, personal conversation, personal requests for help or offers of help, or other services used in the person's own name. These are subject to the Sotsiaal.pro terms of use and privacy terms.

2.5. If a person uses Sotsiaal.pro to prepare their situation and hands over a summary, a pre-inquiry, a document or other selected content to the Organisation, then:

a) the copy received by the Organisation becomes the Organisation's Work Data from the moment of receipt;

b) the rest of the person's private workspace, the source conversation, notes and unsent material do not become the Organisation's Work Data or visible to the Organisation;

c) the Organisation determines the purpose, legal basis, access and retention for the further processing of the copy received;

d) Sotsiaal.pro processes the copy received by the Organisation on behalf of the Organisation only within the scope of the activated workflow.

2.6. The agreement does not give Sotsiaal.pro the right to read from or write to a register, including STAR. A register interface or one-click transfer is part of the service only if it is legally and technically possible and separately agreed in the Application Annex. If the transfer takes place through the User's own register right, login and confirmation, Sotsiaal.pro does not thereby become an authorised user of the register.

2.7. A person's private use sponsored or funded by the Organisation does not automatically fall under this agreement. For such a model, a separate funding or sponsorship annex is concluded, which does not give the Organisation access to the person's private content.

# 3. Pilot, Production Use and activation

3.1. The use of Sotsiaal.pro begins after the Application Annex has been confirmed. The Application Annex specifies at least:

a) whether it is a Pilot or Production Use;

b) the start and term of use;

c) the selected Modules and user roles;

d) the permitted and restricted data categories;

e) the data roles and access rights;

f) the price and payment terms;

g) support and, where necessary, the service level;

h) for a Pilot, the baseline, metrics, success criteria, interim assessment and final decision;

i) the terms for the return, export and deletion of data and for ending the work arrangements.

3.2. Under the same agreement, the Application Annex may convert a Pilot into Production Use. A new main agreement is not needed if the data roles, risk level or applicable law do not change in a way that requires a new agreement.

3.3. A Pilot may take place without a register interface, with limited participants and data and in a technically separated configuration. The smaller scope of a Pilot does not reduce the obligations arising from the Data Protection Law, confidentiality or security.

3.4. A success criterion of a Pilot may address, for example:

a) the time spent on a work task;

b) the number of times the same information is entered repeatedly;

c) the availability of a service or help;

d) clarity for, and the experience of, the user or the person in need of help;

e) the quality of the output and the amount of correction needed;

f) security, privacy and deviations;

g) the financial impact or avoided cost.

3.5. The result of a Pilot does not give an automatic right to Production Use or to a procurement, but the Parties may agree in advance in the Application Annex on a decision path under which a successful Pilot continues as Production Use or moves into a procurement procedure without unjustified duplication of work.

3.6. During a Pilot or Production Use, the Organisation does not have to maintain two parallel work arrangements for longer than is necessary for a safe transition, for verifying the results or for fulfilling a legal obligation. The transition period and the earlier workflow to be discontinued are recorded in the Application Annex.

3.7. A Module that has not been selected in the Application Annex is not part of the service provided to the Organisation. The Organisation's real data are not used in a Module that is hidden, under development or ready for integration before its official activation.

3.8. Sotsiaal.pro may make technical fixes, security updates and changes to the user experience that do not alter the purpose of the processing, the data roles or the Organisation's material rights. A new Module, a new purpose of data use or a material change in risk requires an amendment of the Application Annex.

b) organise thoughts and the work process;

c) draft, improve, translate or structure texts;

d) analyse documents and files;

e) prepare pre-inquiries, summaries, questions and next steps;

f) document field work, the provision of a service or other information arising in the course of work;

g) engage in secure professional cooperation, co-vision, supervision or mentoring;

h) use private reflection and work wellbeing work tools;

i) receive pre-inquiries sent to the Organisation and reply to them;

j) organise and document the provision of home care;

4.6. The exact functional description of the service, the scope of support and the service level for Production Use are specified in the Application Annex. If no service level has been agreed, Sotsiaal.pro provides the service with reasonable care but does not guarantee uninterrupted availability.

When a service profile is published, Sotsiaal.pro compares the name and registry code of the profile with the public data of the Business Register and, for a service subject to a licence requirement, checks the existence of an activity licence in the Register of Economic Activities. 5.6. If the Organisation's internal rule is stricter than the agreement, the Organisation applies the stricter rule, unless the law or a written agreement between the Parties requires otherwise.

# 6. Roles in the processing of personal data

6.1. The Organisation is the controller with respect to the Organisation's Work Data, because the Organisation determines the purpose of the work task, the legal basis, the group of Users and the manner of use.

6.2. Sotsiaal.pro is the processor of the Organisation's Work Data and processes them only on the basis of documented instructions and to the extent necessary for providing the service.

6.3. Documented instructions are the agreement, its annexes, the Application Annex, the Organisation's written instructions, the settings selected by the Organisation and the actions permitted by the User in an activated workflow. Sotsiaal.pro does not carry out an instruction that in its assessment infringes the Data Protection Law before the Parties have resolved the matter, except where the law requires the processing.

6.4. Sotsiaal.pro is a separate controller with respect to data that it processes for a purpose it has determined itself:

a) for managing the user account and authentication;

b) for billing and accounting;

c) for the security of its own service, the prevention of misuse and the defence of legal claims;

d) for providing a private work tool between Sotsiaal.pro and the User, if the Organisation does not determine the purpose of its content and cannot see the content;

e) in another case clearly named in Annex 2.

6.5. The activities of Sotsiaal.pro as a separate controller are described in the Sotsiaal.pro privacy terms. The Organisation is not responsible for the purpose of processing that it does not determine or control.

6.6. If two or more organisations take part in a shared room, co-vision, supervision, mentoring, referral or another shared workflow, joint controllership is not automatically presumed. The actual role of each party is determined according to who determines the purpose and essential means of the specific processing. Where necessary, a separate agreement on the parties' roles and exchange of information is added to the Application Annex.

6.7. If the Parties begin to jointly determine the purpose and essential means of the processing of personal data, they conclude, before starting such processing, a joint controllers agreement in which the obligations are allocated and the essence of the agreement is made available to the data subject.

6.8. If a role specified in Annex 2 or in the Application Annex does not correspond to the actual workflow, the actual activity and the applicable law prevail, and the Parties correct the documentation without undue delay.

6.9. The home care work tool also processes work arrangement data of the Organisation's employees: the visit plan, the time of an absence without the reason, and the date of training and of a background check without the result. The controller of these data is the Organisation as the employer, and Sotsiaal.pro is the processor.

7.1. Sotsiaal.pro:

a) processes the Organisation's Work Data only on the Organisation's documented instructions, including with regard to transfers of data to a third country, unless the processing is required by the law applicable to Sotsiaal.pro;

b) informs the Organisation before any different processing required by law, unless the law prohibits this;

c) ensures that persons authorised to process the data are bound by a confidentiality obligation or are subject to a statutory confidentiality obligation;

d) implements the technical and organisational security measures described in Annex 4;

e) complies with clause 10 when engaging sub-processors;

f) taking into account the nature of the processing, assists the Organisation by appropriate technical and organisational measures in fulfilling its obligations relating to data subjects' rights;

g) assists the Organisation in fulfilling its obligations concerning security, breach notification, impact assessment and, where necessary, prior consultation with the supervisory authority;

h) at the end of the service, deletes or returns the Organisation's Work Data on the Organisation's instruction and deletes existing copies, unless the law requires retention;

i) makes available to the Organisation the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with these obligations, and allows for and supports audits under clause 13;

j) informs the Organisation immediately if it considers that an instruction infringes the Data Protection Law.

7.2. Sotsiaal.pro does not sell the Organisation's Work Data and does not use them to create an advertising profile or for its own or a third party's marketing.

7.3. Sotsiaal.pro does not combine the Organisation's Work Data with another client's work data in a way that enables the other client to see or use the data.

7.4. To assess the reliability, security and usage volume of the service, Sotsiaal.pro may use technical metadata and statistics that do not contain copies of work-related text, documents, conversations, audio or other content. Analytics that can be linked to a person must be necessary, proportionate and described in the privacy terms.

7.5. A permanently anonymised aggregate may be used to develop the quality or reliability of the service or a sector-level view only if neither the person nor the Organisation can be identified by reasonable means. Pseudonymised data are not regarded as anonymous data.

# 8. Use of artificial intelligence

8.1. For the operation of an activated Module, Sotsiaal.pro may use an artificial intelligence service provider to process text, a document, audio or other input, if the service provider is named in Annex 3 and the use complies with the Application Annex.

8.2. Only the content necessary for fulfilling a specific request or function is transmitted to the artificial intelligence service provider. Depending on the Module, this may include:

a) the User's input and the conversation context necessary for preparing a response;

b) the content of a document or file selected by the User;

c) an audio clip for transcription or speech synthesis;

d) instructions, system constraints and technical metadata;

e) a search result or knowledge context necessary for the service to function well.

8.3. Sotsiaal.pro does not use the Organisation's Work Data to train its own artificial intelligence model and does not voluntarily include the Organisation's Work Data in an artificial intelligence service provider's model training, evaluation dataset or data sharing programme.

8.4. Clause 8.3 does not mean that the artificial intelligence service provider does not process data for providing the service, detecting misuse, security or complying with a legal requirement. The service provider's role, the possible retention of application state or of a misuse log, the processing regions and the transfer mechanisms are described in Annex 3, and the account or project configuration used is recorded in the Application Annex.

8.5. If a specific artificial intelligence service allows a stricter data retention setting, regional processing or zero retention, Sotsiaal.pro uses the setting that is agreed in the Application Annex and technically available. Sotsiaal.pro does not confirm zero retention before it has been verified at the service provider's account or project level.

8.6. The Organisation and the User take into account that artificial intelligence output is not automatically true, complete or legally binding. Important output is checked by a human.

8.7. Sotsiaal.pro notifies the Organisation at least 30 calendar days in advance of a change of the main artificial intelligence service provider, unless the change is an urgent security or business continuity measure. In the case of an urgent change, Sotsiaal.pro gives notice at the earliest opportunity.

8.8. If the corresponding check is in use in the service, Sotsiaal.pro checks, with a locally running filter, before the conversation input is transmitted to the artificial intelligence service provider, whether the input contains personal data, and warns the User. The User can switch the check off in their account settings. The check is an aid and does not replace the obligations set out in clauses 5.2 and 5.3.

# 9. Access, private work tools and sharing

9.1. Access is based on the activated Module, the User's role, ownership, participation and a right verified on the server. Hiding something in the user interface alone is not access control.

9.2. A representative of the Organisation does not gain automatic access to a User's conversations, documents, work files or private work tool content. Access must arise from an activated workflow, be technically implemented, legally justified and communicated to the User in a clear manner.

9.3. A User's private work wellbeing or reflection entry remains, by default, the User's alone. The Organisation may be shown only:

a) content knowingly shared by the User themselves; or

b) an aggregate permitted in the Application Annex that is anonymous or has a sufficient number of participants.

9.4. An aggregate view does not give the Organisation the right to demand the original entries, to identify respondents or to draw a work-related, appraisal or personnel conclusion concerning an individual. The minimum sample of the aggregate, the protection of rare responses, the time period, the recipient, the purpose of use and the protection applied in a small team are specified in the Application Annex.

9.5. If an aggregate cannot be anonymised securely enough, it is regarded as personal data, and before use the Parties determine the legal basis, roles, access rights and retention. Sotsiaal.pro does not call an aggregate anonymous merely because the name has been removed.

9.6. In the case of co-vision, supervision and mentoring, at least the following are distinguished:

a) a participant's private notebook;

b) content shared in the participants' shared room;

c) the work data of the supervisor or process facilitator;

d) the proof of participation or of service submitted to the Organisation;

e) possible aggregate metrics.

None of the data types named automatically becomes another.

9.7. The proof of participation submitted to the Organisation contains by default only the minimum information necessary for the service or meeting to take place and for billing, not the substantive discussion of the meeting.

9.8. Sharing initiated by a User or a person must be deliberate and addressed to a specific recipient. Before sending, Sotsiaal.pro displays at least the recipient and the content to be shared and enables the user to confirm the sharing. If the workflow allows an unsent or unopened share to be withdrawn, the withdrawal does not affect a copy that the recipient has already lawfully opened or transferred into their own proceedings.

9.9. In the home care work tool, a client's entries are seen by the client's team and the care coordinator. A covering worker finds the client by name and opens the client's page with a reason; the permission is valid until the end of the Organisation's calendar day and the opening is logged. A worker's concern and an incident of suspected abuse or of a money discrepancy are visible only to the author of the entry and the care coordinator.

9.10. At the intake desk, a pre-inquiry sent to the Organisation is seen only by the employees designated by the Organisation. Declining to accept it and finishing the work require an explanation, which the sender also sees.

9.11. The Organisation takes into account that the sharer sees, next to the content they have shared, on which days it has been opened. The identity of the person who opened it is not shown to the sharer.

# 10. Sub-processors and international data transfers

# Annex 4. Technical and organisational security measures

The measures describe the minimum protection. The Application Annex may require stricter measures. Sotsiaal.pro does not mark a measure as implemented before its actual functioning has been verified.

## 1. Governance and responsibility

Sotsiaal.pro:

a) designates the contacts for the security of the service and for data protection;

b) keeps up-to-date internal instructions on security incidents, access rights, retention and deletion;

c) restricts the access of employees and contractual partners according to the work task and the principle of least privilege;

d) binds persons with access to data by a confidentiality obligation;

e) assesses the data flow and protection needs of a new high-risk Module before activating it.

## 2. Authentication and access

Sotsiaal.pro:

a) uses a user account and role-based access;

b) verifies an ownership, membership, invitation or other access right on the server;

c) does not treat merely being hidden in the user interface as a security measure;

d) stores the secrets necessary for authentication in hashed or otherwise appropriately protected form;

e) restricts administrator access and uses it only for a justified purpose;

f) ends, or makes it possible to end, a user's active sessions in the event of a security risk.

Login takes place with the user account's password or PIN code and with a one-time code or link sent by e-mail. [TO BE VERIFIED BEFORE SIGNING against the actual login flow.] National electronic identification (ID-card, Mobile-ID, Smart-ID) is used only if the platform supports it and it has been agreed in the Application Annex.

## 3. Data minimisation and separation

Sotsiaal.pro:

a) processes only the data necessary for the activated Module;

b) separates the data of users and Organisations by the application's permission checks;

c) does not display private work tool content in the Organisation's administrative view;

d) minimises request content, document content, authentication credentials and other secrets in logs;

e) applies sample and disclosure thresholds in aggregate views and, where necessary, the protection of rare responses;

f) keeps unsent entries of the home care work tool encrypted on the User's device until they reach the server;

g) checks conversation input for personal data with a locally running filter, unless the User has switched the check off in their account settings.

## 4. Transmission and infrastructure

Sotsiaal.pro:

a) uses an encrypted connection for transmission over a public network;

b) keeps production secrets separate from the source code;

c) restricts administrative access to the server;

d) uses supported software versions and applies critical security patches within a reasonable time;

e) uses service providers only in the manner described in Annex 3.

Encryption of data at rest, key management and backup encryption are marked as verified in the Application Annex only after the configuration of the actual infrastructure has been evidenced.

## 5. Logging, monitoring and breach handling

Sotsiaal.pro:

a) logs administrative operations that are significant from a security and data protection perspective;

b) avoids copying substantive work data into the ordinary error log;

c) uses a process for detecting, containing and investigating a security incident and for follow-up measures;

d) retains an audit trail without content for a breach and for deletion jobs;

e) notifies the Organisation in accordance with the procedure set out in clause 12 of the agreement.

## 6. Reliability, backup and recovery

Sotsiaal.pro:

a) backs up the production service in accordance with the procedure described in the Application Annex;

b) restricts access to backups;

c) documents the procedure for recovery and for deleting restored data again;

d) verifies that recovery works at the frequency agreed in the Application Annex;

e) distinguishes the deletion of active data from the life cycle of backups.

The recovery time, the recovery point and the exact retention period of a backup are completed before Production Use is signed.

## 7. Development and change management

Sotsiaal.pro:

a) keeps the development and production environments reasonably separated;

b) does not use real personal data in development or in a Module that has not been activated, unless there is a separately documented basis for this;

c) reviews sensitive changes before they are put into production;

d) tests negative cases of privacy and access, not only visibility in the user interface;

e) keeps new high-risk functions switched off by default until the prerequisites for adoption have been met.

## 8. Deletion and data media

Sotsiaal.pro:

a) uses central workflows for deleting active data and files;

b) monitors failed deletion jobs and tries again to complete them;

c) restricts the restoration of deleted content in ordinary use;

d) securely destroys the data on a data medium taken out of use or uses the service provider's corresponding confirmed process.
